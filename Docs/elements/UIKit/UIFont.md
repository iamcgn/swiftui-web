# UIFont

`Packages/UIKitWeb/Sources/UIKitWebCore/Values/UIFont.swift`; the measured numbers in
`UIFontMetricsTable.swift`, generated by `scripts/uikit-font-metrics-table.py` from
`Fixtures/Goldens/uikit/font-metrics.json` (`scripts/gen-goldens-uikit.sh font-metrics`: UIKit on
Mac Catalyst, every weight at every integer size 6…72 and the eleven text styles, each with the
height a one- and a two-line `UILabel` takes).

## API

`systemFont(ofSize:weight:)`, `boldSystemFont`, `italicSystemFont`, `monospacedSystemFont`,
`monospacedDigitSystemFont` (the plain system font: digits are not measured), `preferredFont(forTextStyle:)`
at the large content size, `init?(name:size:)`, `withSize(_:)` (a plain font at the new size, as
UIKit returns: a text style's metrics belong to its own size), `pointSize`, `familyName`,
`fontName`, `ascender`, `descender`, `capHeight`, `xHeight`, `leading`, `lineHeight`.

## Measured (UIKit, Catalyst, macOS 26.2)

| Metric | Sized system font | Text style |
|---|---|---|
| ascender | 1980/2048 of the size, every weight (16.44 at 17) | its own: body 18.48, headline 18.46, title 31.52, largeTitle 36.96 |
| descender | −432/2048 (−3.59 at 17) | body −5.54, title −9.45 |
| capHeight | 1443/2048 (11.98 at 17) | the sized font's at the style's size |
| xHeight | per weight; 1078/2048 regular and 1101/2048 bold up to 17 pt, lower from 18 pt on (SF's optical sizes; 1040 regular at 28 and up) | body 8.95, headline 9.07 |
| leading | 0 | body 1.98, callout 2.16, footnote 2.69, caption 1.87, caption2 0.04, title 1.02, largeTitle 0.96 |
| lineHeight | round(ascender) + round(−descender): 20 at 17, 16 at 13, 23 at 20 | ascender − descender unrounded: body 24.02, title 40.98, footnote 18.31 |
| one-line UILabel | `lineHeight`, plus one at 6, 7, 10, 11, 15, 16, 19, 20 and 21 pt (24 for 20 pt semibold; the rule is not derivable from the metrics, so it is a table) | rounded up to the pixel: body 24.5, title 41, footnote 18.5; callout 24 (its 22.84 would give 23) |
| line pitch (a further line) | the one-line height | lineHeight + leading, an integer: body 26, callout 25, title 42 |

The text styles are, at the large content size: largeTitle 34, title 28, title2 22, title3 20,
headline 17 semibold, body 17, callout 16, subheadline 15, footnote 13, caption 12, caption2 11.
The `UIFontMetricsTests` suite holds every font the fixtures use to the recorded values.

Open: the metrics of non-integer sizes (the ratios apply; the taller-label sizes are unknown),
custom fonts, italic and monospaced system fonts, the other content size categories.
