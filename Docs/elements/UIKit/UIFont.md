# UIFont

`Packages/UIKitWeb/Sources/UIKitWebCore/Values/UIFont.swift`; the measured numbers in
`UIFontMetricsTable.swift`, generated by `scripts/uikit-font-metrics-table.py` from
`Fixtures/Goldens/uikit/font-metrics.json` (`scripts/gen-goldens-sim.sh uikit font-metrics`: UIKit on
an iPhone SE simulator, iOS 26, every weight at every integer size 6…72 and the eleven text
styles, each with the height a one- and a two-line `UILabel` takes).

## API

`systemFont(ofSize:weight:)`, `italicSystemFont`, `monospacedSystemFont`,
`monospacedDigitSystemFont` (the plain system font: digits are not measured), `preferredFont(forTextStyle:)`
at the large content size, `boldSystemFont` (SF Semibold, as iOS resolves it; `weight: .bold` is Bold), `init?(name:size:)`, `withSize(_:)` (a plain font at the new size, as
UIKit returns: a text style's metrics belong to its own size), `pointSize`, `familyName`,
`fontName`, `ascender`, `descender`, `capHeight`, `xHeight`, `leading`, `lineHeight`.

## Measured (UIKit on an iPhone, iOS 26)

| Metric | Sized system font | Text style |
|---|---|---|
| ascender | 1950/2048 of the size, every weight (16.19 at 17): SF's hhea value | its own: body 18.36, headline 18.33, title 31.30, largeTitle 36.72 |
| descender | −494/2048 (−4.10 at 17) | body −5.93, title −10.11 |
| capHeight | 1443/2048 (11.98 at 17) | the sized font's at the style's size |
| xHeight | per weight; 1078/2048 regular and 1101/2048 bold up to 17 pt, lower from 18 pt on (SF's optical sizes; 1040 regular at 28 and up) | body 8.95, headline 9.07 |
| leading | 0 | body 1.71, callout 1.91, footnote 2.49, caption 1.68, caption2 −0.13, title 0.59, largeTitle 0.43 |
| lineHeight | ascender − descender, unrounded: 20.29 at 17, 15.51 at 13, 23.87 at 20 | ascender − descender: body 24.29, title 41.41, footnote 18.51 |
| a UILabel of n lines | n × lineHeight + (n − 1) × leading, rounded up to the pixel: 20.5 for one 17 pt line, 41 for two, 16 for 13 pt, 24 for 20 pt | the same rule: body 24.5 and 50.5, callout 23.5 and 48.5, title 41.5 and 83.5 |

Every one of the 603 sized entries and the 11 styles follows these rules (`scripts/uikit-font-metrics-table.py`
checks them and fails when a recording does not). Mac Catalyst reports different numbers
(ascender 1980/2048, a rounded line height, labels a point taller at some sizes; the first
recording came from there), which is one reason the goldens moved to the simulator (decision 0015).

The text styles are, at the large content size: largeTitle 34, title 28, title2 22, title3 20,
headline 17 semibold, body 17, callout 16, subheadline 15, footnote 13, caption 12, caption2 11.
The `UIFontMetricsTests` suite holds every font the fixtures use to the recorded values.

Open: custom fonts, italic and monospaced system fonts, the other content size categories.
